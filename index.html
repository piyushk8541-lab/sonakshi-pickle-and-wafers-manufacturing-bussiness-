01 / The Manufacturing Standard

Tradition in every jar,
crunch in every bite.

At Sonakshi Food, our modern manufacturing facility bridges cherished grandma recipes with rigorous food safety standards and scalable production.

Built on purity, hygiene, and authentic Indian flavors.

What began as a passion for time-honored pickle-making has evolved into a state-of-the-art manufacturing unit. We source premium farm-fresh raw mangoes, sun-ripened Kagzi lemons, mountain garlic, and Grade-A Chipsona potatoes directly from partnered agricultural hubs.

Every single batch is processed in stainless-steel grade equipment, seasoned with cold-pressed mustard oil and slow-roasted whole spices, then hermetically packed with nitrogen flush technology to retain factory-fresh crunch and authentic aroma.
